Common questions

How does the free trial work?
Practice, Learn, and Moves unlock with a 7-day free trial when you start a Monthly or Annual plan. Auto-Solve, Stats, and Settings are always free, with no purchase required. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends in Settings → Manage Subscription, and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Manage Subscription" under the Subscription section. This opens Apple's subscription management directly.

I already paid — why is it still locked?
Try "Restore Purchases" in Settings or on the unlock screen. If that doesn't resolve it, email us at the address above and we'll help sort it out.

Is my data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy. Everything is stored locally on your device; nothing is collected or sent to a server.
